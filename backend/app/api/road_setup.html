1. Choose Draw road and click its corners in order. 2. Optionally choose Add lane and click each lane's corners from left to right. 3. Save. Without lane polygons, traffic bands are estimated inside your road. Use convex polygons; lane polygons must be inside the road and must not overlap.

Red: monitored road. Blue: configured lanes. Vehicles outside the road are excluded. Vehicles inside the road but outside a lane remain unassigned. Saving resets current counts; older history remains. The red cross-road counting line is automatic; it is not a physical stop line.
